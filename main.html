This tool helps remove much of the fiddiliness of managing characters in the game Descent: Journeys in the dark. However, this tool does not replace the need for understanding the rules of the game. This tool does not enforce inventory and bag limits, nor does it automate any of the rules of the game such as selling equipment or using health potions. This application simply allows the player to keep track of his or her cards, tokens and general cardboard clutter from their device.

Please load your save file or click the button to create a new party.
